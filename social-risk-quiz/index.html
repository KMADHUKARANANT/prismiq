What the social quiz explores

Social situations can involve belonging, disagreement, and support. This quiz asks you to reflect on common experiences rather than label your personality or predict how others will respond.

Speaking up

How comfortable you feel sharing a different view when a group tends to agree.

Support networks

Whether you feel you have people to turn to during difficult moments.

Social change

How you approach new circles, changing routines, and maintaining relationships.

How to read your result

Your result summarizes your answers using PrismIQ’s scoring rules. It does not diagnose social anxiety, mental health conditions, or relationship problems. See the scoring approach and limitations for details.
