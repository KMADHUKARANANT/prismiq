About PrismIQ

How PrismIQ quiz scoring works

PrismIQ is a set of four short self-reflection quizzes. Each quiz has eight questions, and answer options contribute 1, 3, or 5 points to a category total. The app compares that total with the result ranges for that category.

How the score is calculated

  1. Choose one answer for each question in a category quiz.
  2. Each selected answer adds its assigned point value to that quiz’s total.
  3. The app compares the total with that category’s result thresholds and displays the matching profile.
  4. The category result shows points earned out of the quiz maximum. Its bar is a visual indicator of that score.

The home screen’s overall percentage is the total points from completed quizzes divided by their combined maximum possible points. It is a simple summary of those responses, not a separately validated measure.

What the result can and cannot tell you

A result can help you reflect on the answers you selected. It is not a prediction, diagnosis, validated psychological instrument, financial recommendation, health assessment, or measure of someone’s worth or ability.

Results depend on self-reported answers and the scoring rules currently in the app. They may not capture your full circumstances, and different answers or circumstances can lead to a different profile.
