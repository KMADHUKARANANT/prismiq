        🌐 Overall Risk Score

        What does this overall score mean?
        This percentage combines your results across all completed quizzes (Career, Financial, Social, and Physical) to show your overall comfort with risk.

        Score Ranges:

        • 0% – 35% (Play It Safe): You prefer predictability, clear safety nets, and steady routines across life.
        • 36% – 65% (Balanced Explorer): You take calculated chances when it makes sense, while keeping safeguards in place.
        • 66% – 100% (Bold Adventurer): You are very comfortable with big changes, high uncertainty, and fast-paced opportunities.

        💡 How to Read Your Result

        1. Your Profile Name: Your title shows your main approach to handling risk and uncertainty in this area.

        2. Score Range (8 to 40 points):

        • 8 – 18 (Cautious): You prioritize stability, safety, and protecting what you already have.
        • 19 – 29 (Balanced): You take planned, smart leaps when the potential reward is worth it.
        • 30 – 40 (Bold): You thrive on change, high stakes, and new challenges.
