      🌐 Composite Prism Propensity

      What is Composite Propensity?
      This percentage reflects your overall baseline risk tolerance aggregated across all completed domain audits (Career & Strategic, Financial & Wealth, Social & Conformity, Adventure & Physical).

      Interpretation Bands:

      • 0% – 35% (Conservative): You naturally seek high predictability, structural safety, and risk mitigation across life domains.
      • 36% – 65% (Balanced Explorer): You maintain calculated flexibility, taking measured risks in specific domains while retaining safeguards in others.
      • 66% – 100% (High Velocity): You possess an aggressive risk tolerance, comfortably navigating high uncertainty, rapid change, and volatility.

      💡 How to Read Your Result

      1. Archetype Persona: Your assigned title represents your primary behavioral orientation under uncertainty for this domain.

      2. Propensity Score: Calculated on a scale from 8 to 40 across 8 scenario checks:

      • 8 – 18 (Low Risk Threshold): Defensive posture prioritizing stability, security, and capital/reputation preservation.
      • 19 – 29 (Moderate / Calculated): Strategic balance taking calculated leaps only when asymmetric upside exists.
      • 30 – 40 (High Propensity): Dynamic risk-taker who thrives on chaos, high volatility, and non-conformity.
