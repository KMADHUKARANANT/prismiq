What the financial quiz explores

The questions invite you to think about the buffers and obligations that affect your comfort with financial uncertainty. They are not a review of your accounts, assets, or personal circumstances.

Emergency planning

Whether you have considered how unexpected bills might affect your available savings.

Debt and spending

How regular obligations and spending habits relate to your sense of financial flexibility.

Investment exposure

How you think about concentration and potential losses, without telling you what to buy or sell.

How to read your result

Your result summarizes your answers using PrismIQ’s scoring rules. It is not a measure of creditworthiness, investment suitability, or future financial security. Read the scoring approach and limitations for more context.
