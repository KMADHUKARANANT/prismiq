Explore your career risk profile

Reflect on how you respond to changes in your role, industry, skills, and work stability. PrismIQ’s career quiz is a short self-assessment to help you notice patterns—not a prediction of your career outcomes.

Start the career quiz

Your result is a reflection prompt. It does not replace career, legal, or financial advice.

What the career quiz explores

The assessment focuses on your comfort with change and the habits that can affect adaptability at work. It asks you to consider the ways your skills, role, and professional support may evolve over time.

Skills and learning

How actively you refresh skills as tools, technology, or industry expectations change.

Role and industry shifts

How you think about changes that may affect the stability or relevance of your current work.

Career support

Whether your professional connections and opportunities extend beyond one project or workplace.

How to read your result

Your result summarizes your answers using PrismIQ’s scoring rules. Treat it as a snapshot of your responses today, not an objective measure of employability or a forecast. You can review the scoring approach and limitations before taking the quiz.
